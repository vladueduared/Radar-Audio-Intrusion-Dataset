# Ethics and Informed Consent

The study received favorable approval from the Ethics Committee of the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca.

**Ethics approval:** No. 841/16.09.2026  
**Related ethics application:** CEU 840/11.09.2026

The Ethics Committee assessed the research activities as non-invasive and confirmed that the study protocol included prior information of the participants, informed consent, and compliance with the applicable principles for the protection of personal data.

Informed consent for participation and for the research use and public release of the collected radar and audio data was obtained from all participants involved in the study.

The public dataset contains:

- radar macro-detection recordings;
- stereo audio recordings;
- event annotations;
- session-level metadata;
- supporting documentation.

No video recordings are included in the public dataset.

The public release of this repository was made following ethics approval.
